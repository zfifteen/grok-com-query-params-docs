Shortcut encoding tip

In Apple Shortcuts: URL Encode the prompt text, then Combine Text with base https://grok.com/?q=. Open with Open URLs. That is a GET — no POST body.

Harness tip

Always open the user’s Safari (or Chrome profile) that is already logged into grok.com. Embedded browser sessions are often signed out and will not match SuperGrok / Private behavior.

Private ordering

Browsers treat everything after # as a fragment. Write ?q=…&mode=expert#private — never put query parameters after the hash.

URL length

Giant diffs and full logs blow past practical URL limits. Let Hermes summarize or truncate locally first, then deep-link the compressed brief.
